Video Game Localizations

I've always targeted video games that are considered indie and have at least some modding evidence by the fanbase, and the existing mods don't necessarily have to be about localization.

Undertale

Interestingly enough, this game's localization started AFTER the initiation of the localization of its successor game, Deltarune. Idk, Deltarune was the game I was fixated on at the time I realized I could make Mongolian localizations.

Undertale Yellow

To be honest, this one barely counts as a localization, and more like a slight modification.

Deltarune

The first video game localization I've worked on, and the one that has the most progress so far.

Oneshot

A rather new localization and I have a hunch that it won't be as popular as the other localizations. Nevertheless, it is on par with the soul, charm, and effort of the other indie video games I've chosen to start making localizations for.

Doki Doki Literature Club

I deeply appreciate how the meta the game can be and break the 4th wall so effectively. Especially how the player is motivated to interact with the game files and find things that intuitively should not be there. And also the art and music are really well done. I really want Mongolian players to also experience the same immersion and subversion and be horrified when the in-game character suddenly calls out your real name.
